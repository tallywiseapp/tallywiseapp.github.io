# Tallywise

A simple personal finance tracker: inflow and outflow, accounts and cards, loans, recurring payments, savings goals, and investments. Every currency is tallied on its own and never converted.

Open it at https://iampaulohhh.github.io/tallywise/ and use "Add to Home Screen" on your phone to install it like an app.

Your data stays on your device (and in your own Google Drive if you connect it). Nothing is sent to this repository.
